About me

My name is Yuze Fu, or you can call me Lucas. I am a fourth-year undergraduate student in the Department of Computer and Mathematical Science at the University of Toronto Scarborough. I double major in Mathematics and Computer Science, aiming for a PhD degree as my short-term goal. I am currently interested in Physics-based Animation, Geometry Processing and Numerical Methods for PDEs.

"读万卷书，行万里路。 -- 司马迁 《史记》". This sentence is from a Chinese history book writen 2000 years ago, meaning that true wisdom is born from the union of deep knowledge gained through study and profound understanding earned from real-world experience. It reminds me to learn from things besides books.

Gaming also plays an important role in my life. I mainly play two types of game: Sandbox and RPG. My favourite Sandbox game is Minecraft which has accompanied me since 2012. Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild is my best RPG game, still fascinating me time after 5 times.
